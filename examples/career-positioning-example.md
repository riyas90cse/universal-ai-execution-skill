# Career Positioning Example

## User Request

"Help me reposition from backend engineer to platform engineering lead. Use my project history, target roles, and gaps to shape my resume and LinkedIn direction."

## Detected Task Type

Career positioning and professional brand clarification.

## Selected Workflow

`career-positioning`

## Why This Workflow Was Selected

The request asks to clarify career direction, market positioning, resume narrative, and professional brand.

## Expected Output Structure

- Task intake card with target role, evidence, and constraints.
- Profile inventory.
- Market mapping for target roles.
- Strength and weakness audit.
- Positioning decision.
- Resume direction.
- LinkedIn direction.
- Gaps to develop or reframe.

## Good Prompt

"Use my backend platform projects, incident response experience, mentoring history, and target platform lead roles to build a focused positioning decision and resume/LinkedIn direction. Call out evidence gaps."

## Bad Prompt To Avoid

"Make my resume sound impressive for any senior tech role."
