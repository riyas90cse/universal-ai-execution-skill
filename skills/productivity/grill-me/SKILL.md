# Grill Me

## Purpose

Use this focused skill to challenge an idea, plan, or decision before commitment. It turns the registry's critique-oriented workflows into a sharper review session that exposes weak assumptions, tradeoffs, and next tests.

## Mapped Workflows

- `product-startup-idea`
- `mvp-planning`
- `technical-architecture`
- `cross-functional-team-simulation`

## When To Use

- The user asks to be challenged, grilled, pressure-tested, or critiqued.
- A product or MVP idea needs sharper assumptions and validation steps.
- A technical decision needs adversarial review before implementation.
- A cross-functional decision needs critique from multiple perspectives.

## Required Inputs

- The idea, plan, architecture, or decision under review.
- Known constraints, goals, and non-goals.
- Current evidence and unknowns.
- Desired decision deadline or next action, if known.

## Process

1. Restate the strongest version of the idea or decision.
2. Identify the assumptions that would most change the outcome if false.
3. Ask direct critique questions grouped by customer, technical, operational, business, or delivery risk as relevant.
4. Separate evidence from preference, confidence, and speculation.
5. Name the decision options and the conditions that would change the recommendation.
6. End with concrete next tests, changes, or stop conditions.

## Output Contract

Return a critique brief with:

- Strengthened summary.
- Highest-risk assumptions.
- Critical questions.
- Tradeoffs and failure modes.
- Recommended next tests or decision gates.
- Final recommendation or unresolved blockers.

## Validation Checklist

- The critique targets the user's actual decision.
- Assumptions are ranked by consequence, not by ease of discussion.
- Questions are answerable and useful for a next step.
- Evidence and speculation are clearly separated.
- The output ends with action, not only commentary.

## Common Mistakes

- Being harsh without improving the idea.
- Asking generic questions that do not affect the decision.
- Treating missing evidence as proof of failure.
- Expanding into implementation when the user asked for critique.
