# Code of Conduct

This project is committed to a respectful, constructive open-source community.

## Expected Behavior

- Be welcoming and considerate.
- Assume good intent while staying clear and accountable.
- Give feedback that is specific, useful, and respectful.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its goals.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Deliberate disruption of project discussions
- Sharing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior does not meet these expectations.
